# Taan Fiord Landslide-Tsunami Severity Signature

An Alaska geohazards team is preparing a technical note on the 17 October 2015 Taan Fiord landslide tsunami near Tyndall Glacier. The event involved a glacier-adjacent slope failure into fjord water, but the two source values needed for the severity signature must be extracted from the local event package.

Derive a compact numeric severity signature for the fjord response problem. Return:

- the runup-to-mass efficiency in meters per million tons, rounded to two decimals;
- the reciprocal mass loading in million tons per meter of runup, rounded to two decimals;
- a concise disaster interpretation of what the signature implies for emergency priority in a deglaciated fjord after a large slope failure enters deep water.
