# Tropical Cyclone Freddy Persistence-Rainfall Ledger

A climate-risk analyst is checking the February-March 2023 record for Tropical Cyclone Freddy as it crossed the southern Indian Ocean and later affected Madagascar, Mozambique, and Malawi. The decision is whether the numbers support a long-duration, multi-pulse rainfall-and-exposure case rather than a short isolated rainfall burst.

Return a compact JSON object with `label`, `passed_tests`, `storm_days`, `window_days`, `storm_window_fraction`, `event_total_mm`, `max_72h_mm`, `max_72h_share`, `grid_peak_mm`, `grid_peak_ratio`, `population_million`, and `isolated_72h_burst_rejection`. For local rainfall, use the package point-weather hourly precipitation series over the locked event window. Apply these four tests: long duration if storm-status duration is at least 30 days and the inclusive analysis window is at least 35 days; distributed rainfall if the wettest 72-hour total is under 35% of the local event total while the local event total exceeds 300 mm; gridded precipitation agreement if the larger gridded peak is at least 180 mm and the larger-to-smaller gridded peak ratio is at most 1.10; population loading if the compact population total is at least 5.0 million. Put the short explanation of why the isolated 72-hour burst summary fails in `isolated_72h_burst_rejection`.
