# Henan Flood Mechanism Diagnosis

A provincial flood-analysis lead is reviewing the July 17-23, 2021 Henan extreme rainfall and flooding in and around Zhengzhou. The immediate question is which hazard mechanism should lead the after-action diagnosis and near-term planning narrative: rapid urban pluvial flooding, slower basin-wide agricultural inundation, coastal surge, or retrospective landscape mapping.

Prepare a concise technical diagnosis that identifies the leading mechanism and explains why it is the strongest interpretation. Anchor the diagnosis with the rainfall concentration, population scale, and impact pathway that matter most for life-safety action, and be explicit about how broad visual or land-surface context should and should not influence the conclusion.

Return a short structured answer with:

- `diagnosis_label`: compact mechanism-priority label.
- `priority_rationale`: brief explanation of the hazard trigger and impact pathway.
- `key_anchors`: small set of quantitative anchors that matter most for the diagnosis.
- `response_chain`: three short phrases connecting trigger, flood process, and operational priority.
- `imagery_role`: one sentence on how broad visual or land-surface context should affect the diagnosis.
