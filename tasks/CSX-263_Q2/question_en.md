# Carbon Monoxide Amplification and Response Triage

During the September-October 2015 Indonesian drought, peat-fire, and transboundary haze crisis, a response team needs a short technical note on the Borneo smoke phase. The decision is how strongly the reported surface carbon monoxide anomaly should shift attention from generic hazard response toward the most relevant operational priority for this compound event.

Using the event documentation, compute the amplification ratio between the high 2015 surface carbon monoxide concentration reported for parts of Borneo and the usual average concentration over Indonesia. Round the ratio to one decimal place. Then identify the response priority implied by that ratio, tying the numeric result to the peat-fire haze mechanism, population-exposure context, and any caution about overinterpreting a burn-scar-only interpretation.

Return:
- `CO ratio: ...x`
- `Priority: ...`
- A concise 2-3 sentence explanation of the mechanism-impact chain and why the priority is stronger than burn-scar-only or unrelated storm-response interpretations.
